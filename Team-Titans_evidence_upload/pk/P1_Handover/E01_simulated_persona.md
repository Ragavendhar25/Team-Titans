# E01 — SIMULATED persona interview (instructor-provided, not primary research)
Illustrative answers modelled on a Legal Metrology inspector. Do not cite as real findings.
1. Last check: visit to a mid-sized store; sample of 15-20 food/personal-care packs; check declarations (name/address, common name, net quantity, date, MRP, consumer-care contact, origin); note violations in a register; photograph evidence; report.
2. Time: 5-8 min per clean label, 15+ min if complex; most of a day per shop. Slowed by small print, curved packs, multi-language labels, stickered MRPs, retyping notes.
3. Missed/redone: missed consumer-care number; blurry photos redone; font height mis-measured on curved pack; reports sent back for incomplete details.
4. Hard labels: ask a colleague or use a translator app; refer up to the office, delaying the case by days.
