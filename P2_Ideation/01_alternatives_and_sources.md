- **A1 Existing OCR tools (open-source, cloud APIs):** read text in many languages. Gap: not tuned to Legal Metrology rules; accuracy on curved/stickered packs unverified locally. Source: vendor documentation (capability only; add title, URL and access date here).
- **A2 Printed pack-size checklist + training:** cheap, offline, no IT approval. Gap: still manual; no photo evidence or report draft. Source: to check with inspectors; not yet evidenced.
- **Rules:** Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules, 2011; Legal Metrology Act, 2009.
